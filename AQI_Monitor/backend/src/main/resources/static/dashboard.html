Primary Pollution Sources

Vehicular exhaust, industrial emissions, construction dust, agricultural crop residue burning, and ambient dust drive seasonal AQI variations.

📊

National AQI Standard

The Indian National Air Quality Index aggregates 8 core pollutants (PM2.5, PM10, NO2, SO2, CO, O3, NH3, Pb) on a 0–500 numerical scale.

💡

Air Quality Categories

0–50 Good, 51–100 Satisfactory, 101–200 Moderate, 201–300 Poor, 301–400 Very Poor, and 401–500 Severe environmental health levels.
