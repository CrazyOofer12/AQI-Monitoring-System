Major Pollution Sources in India

Vehicular emissions, industrial pollution, crop burning, construction dust, and biomass burning are primary contributors to poor air quality.

📊

Indian AQI Standards

India follows the National Air Quality Index with 8 pollutants: PM2.5, PM10, NO₂, SO₂, CO, O₃, NH₃, and Pb. AQI ranges from 0-500.

⏰

Peak Pollution Times

Typically highest during winter months (Nov-Feb), morning rush hours (7-10 AM), and evening (6-9 PM). Diwali season sees severe spikes.
